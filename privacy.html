Privacy

A review preview.
A clear data policy.

This review site may be viewed locally or on our private preview host. The enquiry form does not send your details, and no SEAQR analytics are enabled.

How this preview works

Most videos, images, scripts, and styles are served from this website. The buoy video and aerial demonstrations use external Vimeo players and connect to Vimeo only when you choose to open them. Vimeo handles requests to that player under its own policies. The enquiry form prepares a browser-only review; copying or saving an enquiry is initiated by you.

This site does not store form data in browser storage or transmit it to SEAQR. Any enquiry file you choose to save remains on your device. A session-only motion preference is stored if you use the footer motion toggle. The private preview host handles authentication and web requests under its own policies. A production privacy notice and contact delivery service will be added before the company website launches.

Media and examples

Recorded model examples illustrate selected behavior, with context shown in each player. They are not live surveillance feeds. Illustrative port photography is not presented as a SEAQR installation.
